Sprout design spec

Mascot sheet, colour and type spec with contrast ratios, and the component list.

1. Mascot sheet

The mascot is a geometric mark: one circle, one half-disc of soil, one stem and two leaf shapes. No face, no limbs, no outline. It expresses feeling only through growth stage and a slow sway. Each pose is inline SVG (about 1 KB). Motion is CSS only: leaves sway ±5° over 4.5 s, and the stem grows in once (0.9 s). Total animation payload is under 1 KB of CSS, against a 100 KB budget.

2. Colour

Contrast ratios are calculated with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula. Body text needs 4.5:1, large text (24px, or 19px bold) and UI components need 3:1.

Pair (foreground on background)UseRatioNeedsResult

Amber #F2B84B is used only for the mascot's bud, which is decorative and never carries meaning on its own, so it is not in the table.

Font stack: system-ui, "Segoe UI", "Noto Sans", "Noto Sans Devanagari", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif. No web-font download, so the type costs 0 KB and renders at once on low-end devices. Hindi falls through to the device's Devanagari font.
