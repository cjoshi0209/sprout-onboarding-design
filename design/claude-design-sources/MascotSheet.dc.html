Why it does not read as a cartoon

  • No face, no limbs, no outline, no speech bubble.
  • Two greens plus ink. One amber bud, only in the Done pose.
  • Flat fills and true geometry: arcs and straight lines only.
  • It never talks. Copy always comes from the interface.
  • It doubles as the logo, so it stays a brand mark.

Motion spec

  • Leaves sway ±5° over 4.5 s, ease in-out, alternating.
  • The stem grows in once over 0.9 s.
  • CSS only. Each pose is about 1 KB of SVG, far under the 100 KB budget.
  • All motion stops under reduced-motion. That leaves the static poses above, so there is no second asset to maintain.
  • Minimum size 24 px. Clear space equal to the stem width on every side.
