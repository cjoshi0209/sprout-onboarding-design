Type, tokens and components

Standard parts, one front-end developer, three weeks

Everything here maps to a native HTML element plus a small set of tokens. No component library and no web-font beyond one family, so the page stays light on a ₹10,000 phone over 4G.

Illustrative strings, to be reviewed by a native speaker. Buttons grow with their text, nothing has a fixed width, and line heights of 1.5 to 1.6 stop matras from clipping. Plan for about 30% longer strings.

Three-week build plan

Week 1Tokens, type, shell, disclaimer bar, buttons, fields, mascot SVG. Homepage on mobile and desktop.
Week 2Screens 1 to 3: welcome, checklist with reminder, details form with validation and masking.
Week 3Screens 4 and 5, all verification states, notify-me, then an accessibility, 4G performance and Hindi pass.

Budget: page weight under 1.5 MB (one font family plus inline SVG, no images), animation CSS only and well under 100 KB, 6.2 s on 4G is the number to beat.
